# Image assets

Three original AI-generated product photographs made with the built-in imagegen tool for this illustrative design. Not photographs of actual stock.

- `dist/assets/garden.webp`: daisy-shaped resin clip pair, 1536 × 1024.
- `dist/assets/heart.webp`: pink heart clip pair, 1254 × 1254.
- `dist/assets/lavender.webp`: floral resin clip, 1254 × 1254.

Source PNGs were converted to WebP at quality 88, preserving composition. Product and hero crops are CSS object-fit only.

Google Fonts are loaded with system-font fallbacks: Cormorant Garamond, Noto Serif TC, Noto Sans TC.
